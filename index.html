About

I work on production RL for long-horizon agents: environments, trajectory-level evaluation, real-world interaction signals, and post-training systems.

Previously, I was on the Llama research team, working across pretraining, architecture experiments, distributed training, post-training, and long-context systems. My public contributions include Llama 2–4, the 128K-context Llama 3.1, 10M+ context work for Llama 4, and MLGym for AI research agents.
